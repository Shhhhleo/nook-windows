# Nook appearance templates

Download one of the JSON files, then choose **Settings → Appearance → Import template**.

| Template | Expanded music | Resting strip | Desktop style |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Everyday | 840 × 132 | 700 × 42 | Glass |
| Focus | 760 × 132 | 620 × 40 | Paper |
| Studio | 960 × 140 | 800 × 44 | Midnight |

All sizes are logical pixels. These presets are also included in Nook, so no download or account is required to use them. Save your own through **Save template**. Templates change appearance only and do not include tasks, accounts, photos or desktop positions.
